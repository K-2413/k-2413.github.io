Wow, I created and launched my first website, and it was really hard work! However, it was fun, and I think I've created a good website that reflects my personality. Originally, I was in the Electronic and Mechanical Engineering department at a technical high school. The Electronic and Mechanical Engineering department is a department where you can learn about "mechanics," "electricity," and "information" in a broad but shallow way. I was particularly good at electricity. Electricity has two aspects: "high-voltage electricity" from power plants to household outlets, and "low-voltage electricity" from outlets to electronic devices (smartphones, computers, etc.). Of these, I liked "semiconductors," which are low-voltage electricity. But what inspired me to create this website, or rather, to pursue information technology, was Apple's official website. Its simplicity and ease of use really impressed me. Since then, I've been slowly learning HTML and CSS. My ultimate goal is to create "the best website in the world."
